What the data can tell you

As you record more days, LifeMaxing will describe patterns gently. It won't label a difficult week as failure or treat mood as a productivity score.

Patterns are clues, not diagnoses. A low mood score is a prompt to notice what was happening around you — sleep, workload, people, health, or circumstances — not a judgement about you.

PRIVACY & SECURITY

Your private archive

LifeMaxing encrypts your saved archive on this device using your passcode. If you forget the passcode, there is no reset link — restore from a backup only if you have one and know its passcode.

Backup & restore

Export a backup regularly and keep a copy somewhere separate, such as Google Drive. Importing a backup replaces the current archive.

CLOUD BACKUP · GOOGLE

Google sign-in & cloud sync

The app is currently local-first. Google cloud sync needs a Firebase project and your own Google sign-in configuration. I have not connected an account or claimed cloud backup is active. Your encrypted export works now; cloud sync is the next integration step once the project credentials are configured.
